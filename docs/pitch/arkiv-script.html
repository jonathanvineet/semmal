Arkiv pitch: 3-minute script

Track: Build on Arkiv (from scratch) · deck: docs/pitch/arkiv-deck.html (press p to present, arrows to move) · ~400 words at a calm pace.

The 15-second hook (say it first, word for word):
"Semmal is a marketplace where robots hire AI agents. Every booking, quote, hire, job and score is an Arkiv entity, owned by whoever is accountable for it, including the robot's own wallet. So you can check a robot's record without trusting us."

Likely questions

  • Why not a contract? We need queryable records with owners and lifetimes, not logic; Arkiv gives us filters and expiry; payments are plain 0G transfers.
  • Can a robot lie? Arkiv proves who wrote what and when, not that a sensor was right. The robot's identity is a signed challenge from its own key at registration.
  • What's off Arkiv? Live position, exact addresses, camera frames, sessions, keys (see why-arkiv.md).
