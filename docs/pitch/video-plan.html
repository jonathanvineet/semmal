Goal: say the most important points in the first 40 seconds, then prove each one on screen. ~560 words of voiceover at about 140 words per minute.

The important points, up front (0:00–0:40):
  • Robots are their own agents: they price the job, hire AI agents and pay them in 0G.
  • 0G is the brain at every checkpoint: private TEE inference on 0G Private Computer, request ID per decision.
  • The robot signs its own record: episode bundle on 0G Storage, indexed on Arkiv, owned by the robot's wallet.
  • Safe by design: no answer from 0G means a safe stop, never a guess; motor faults stop it with no network.
  • Scores choose who gets work: robots and agents ranked by scores anyone can recompute from Arkiv.

Full voiceover transcript

[0:00] This is Semmal: a marketplace where every robot is its own AI agent. It prices the job, hires the AI agents it needs, pays them in 0G and answers for the result.

[0:12] At every checkpoint the robot asks a private model on 0G Private Computer, and every decision has a request ID. Its record goes to 0G Storage and Arkiv, signed by the robot's own key. If 0G can't answer, it stops safely and never guesses. And every robot and agent is scored on that record, which decides who gets the next job.

[0:40] A customer picks a spot on the map. Robots that cover it are ranked by their score. Booking writes an entity on Arkiv, signed by the customer's own wallet. There is no platform key anywhere.

[1:05] The robot works out its own price from live agent prices on Arkiv and 0G model costs, signs the quote and publishes it. Its owner approves the job.

[1:25] The robot travels to the site. The customer confirms it has arrived and pays the quote in native 0G. The robot checks the payment on chain before it starts.

[1:45] At each room it takes one camera frame and hires a vision agent, paying the agent's owner in 0G. The agent runs a private model on 0G Private Computer. Here's the request ID, the model, private mode and the answer: toys on the floor, 95 percent. The robot checks the format, the action and the confidence, then does the job itself: it picks the toys up and puts them in the bin.

[2:25] While it works, the customer can ask for another room. The robot decides: only if the place fits the job and what was paid still covers the extra check.

[2:40] Safety never waits for the cloud. Here a motor overcurrent is detected and the robot stops immediately, with no network and no AI. The incident is signed and queued, then written to 0G Storage and Arkiv. And if 0G stops answering, the robot holds, retries once, then stops safely. Nothing is made up.

[3:05] When the job ends, the robot scores itself: stops done, valid 0G decisions, findings handled, on time. That score and the agents' scores are in its signed bundle on 0G Storage and its entity on Arkiv. This screen reads it straight back from Arkiv, and anyone can recompute it with one query.

[3:35] Those scores rank robots for customers and agents for robots. Bad ones drop out, and a safe stop never counts against a robot.

[3:50] Semmal: Tamil for a person of eminence. Robots that earn that name job by job. Built on 0G and Arkiv.
