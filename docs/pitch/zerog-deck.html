The problem

Robots now act on what an AI tells them. Nobody can see what it was shown, who paid, or whether the decision was real.

Camera images go to a model you can't inspect. When the cloud AI is slow or down, a robot either guesses or freezes. And the record of what it decided lives in the operator's logs.
