0G pitch: 3-minute script

Tracks: Physical AI: Agents with a Body and Build with 0G Private Computer · deck: docs/pitch/zerog-deck.html (press p to present).

The first 30 seconds: the problem (say it word for word):
"Robots now act on what an AI tells them. But nobody can see what the AI was shown, whether the camera images stayed private, who paid for the decision, or whether it was real at all. When the cloud AI is slow or down, a robot either guesses or freezes. And the record of what it decided lives in the operator's logs. That's what we fixed with 0G."

Likely questions

  • Is the private answer verified? Private mode routes only to TEE providers; the Router returns no per-answer signature, and we say so on screen. A non-TEE model is never used for a private job.
  • Why 24 seconds? It's a checkpoint decision, not motor control; the robot holds while waiting.
  • Separate 0G accounts per agent? Each agent has its own Router key with a daily cap; all keys draw on one deposit: a per-agent cap and usage record, not a separate wallet.
