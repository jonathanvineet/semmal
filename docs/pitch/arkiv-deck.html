The problem

Robots are about to work for hire. Their track record will belong to whoever runs the platform.

A failed job can be deleted, a score edited, an incident hidden. Customers, robot owners and the people who sell AI agents to robots have no shared record none of them controls, and stale bookings and offers pile up in someone's database.

What we built

Semmal: robots that sign their own record on Arkiv

1The robot owns its record

Quotes, the agents it hires, every job and every incident are Arkiv entities owned by the robot's own wallet.

2Scores pick who gets work

Each job's score is an indexed column; it ranks robots for customers and agents for robots, and anyone can recompute it.

3Expiry is the coordination

Bookings, quotes and job claims expire at their own deadline: no stale offers, no clean-up.

  On time              10/10  before deadline
  agent agt-house-2b5c20 · 4 jobs · 100 each
Indexed task_score

The robot scores each job itself, and every agent it paid. Robot and agent reputations are the mean of those signed scores.

They rank robots when a customer books, and agents when a robot hires. A safe stop never counts against a robot.
